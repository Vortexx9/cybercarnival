FREQUENT QUESTIONS

CYBER CARNIVAL is open to students, developers, researchers, ethical hackers, and security enthusiasts of all skill levels. If you are passionate about security, code, and debugging infrastructure, you are welcome to enter the battlefield.

The CTF runs as a continuous jeopardy-style hacking challenge focused on exploiting specifically vulnerable systems. The Hackathon is a product development sprint where teams build utility prototypes to address critical defensive challenges. You may participate in either or both, provided schedules permit.

Registration and entry are completely free. You must bring your own development terminal (laptop) equipped with standard penetration testing tools or virtualization software suitable for CTF payloads.

We feature a comprehensive cash prize pool for top-performing teams across the CTF and Hackathon, alongside targeted Bug Bounty payouts for approved security disclosures in our designated test-range sandbox.
